The agents agreed that the water hero (azure ribbons wrapping the handstand over cotton clouds) was the strongest image in every style. This round tests that direction rather than widening it:

- **31 ink ladder.** The same water hero at ink strengths 1 to 5. Telling me a number is the fastest way to steer.

- **34 water close-ups.** A developer reference sheet showing each part of the water effect: body, foam crest, curls, droplets, tails, crossings and splash.
