Every prompt = 'Using your image generation tool, generate this image and save the PNG into the current directory as <NN-name>.png. Report the final path.' + SCENE + COMMON STYLE (below).

## COMMON STYLE (appended to every prompt)
STYLE: Original artwork, a website homepage design mockup in the visual language of anime production drawings (genga key-frame pencils and douga). Everything lives on soft pastel animation paper sheets (cream / pale lilac / pale butter) with a row of three peg-bar holes punched at the top edge of each sheet, circled frame numbers in the corner, timing marks, X-sheet style ruled grids, in-between charts (tick-mark ladders), registration crosses. Drawing: loose rough graphite pencil plus coloured pencil, light-blue and soft-red construction lines (circles for joints, centre lines, perspective cross lines) with cleaner darker line on top, faint onion-skin ghost copies of previous frames in lilac and peach. Delicate soft pastel watercolour wash colour (lilac, periwinkle, peach, cream, butter) and loosely sketched dreamy pastel clouds. The reference images give the mood: the handstand figure (man upside down on hands, blue jeans, maroon sweater, green beanie, sneakers up) and the dreamy lilac/peach cloud sky. Bright, airy, soft and gentle; NOT dark, NOT paper-collage, NOT crayon zine, no neon, absolutely NO green accents anywhere in the UI (the beanie may be a muted teal only inside the drawing). Handwritten pencil-like lettering, dark indigo/graphite text, crisp and legible.
