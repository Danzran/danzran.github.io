## Source and prompts

All drafts use the supplied `handstand.PNG` as the mandatory subject/pose reference and the built-in image_gen tool. The original source is unchanged. The exact prompts are in **PROMPTS.md**.

These are flattened concept PNGs, not layered Photoshop documents or final masks. Generated representations can vary in small clothing or anatomical details; a production composition should use a carefully prepared cutout of the actual source image once the visual direction is chosen.

## What to compare

Look separately at the body treatment, surrounding material, palette, typography, and motion idea. A favorite component from one image can be combined with another direction; choosing a draft does not mean accepting every detail.

Use the numbers when referring to a draft. The images deliberately range from light and painterly to dark and metallic, with graphic and minimal alternatives.

The scrolling camera idea is described in **CAMERA-PATH.md**. This round explores it visually without replacing the existing site.
