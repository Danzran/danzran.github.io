# Finding the cloud + water + drawn-effects blend

Updated direction: Cotton Sky is the closest mockup so far. Add smooth flowing water and curling mist, with the broad expressive shapes of elemental animation. Keep the palette composed and less pink-dominated. The goal is refined, fluid, atmospheric motion with some authored drawing variation.

**Best starting search phrase: stylized elemental VFX.** This is a useful umbrella, not the name of one exact style. The intended result combines several kinds of shape, material, and timing.

## Useful terms

| Search phrase | What to look for | How it relates to this portfolio |
|---|---|---|
| Stylized elemental VFX | Drawn or sculpted water, wind, smoke, fire, and energy | The strongest broad category for the new direction |
| Anime water effects / toon water VFX | Curving blue bodies, white foam bands, simplified shading | The flowing shapes suggested by the Demon Slayer reference |
| Water ribbons / ribbon trails | Long connected shapes with sweeping arcs and tapered ends | A swing trail, cursor response, or moving section connector |
| Hand-drawn FX animation / 2D FX flipbook | Sequences of authored effect drawings | The framey quality in moving water, splashes, and foam |
| Stylized mist / smoke wisps | Curling soft forms that stretch and dissolve | A bridge between the fluffy cloud world and flowing water |
| Flowmap distortion | An image or texture warped along a moving flow field | A subtle way to make painted scenery feel liquid in a browser |
| Fluid advection | Color or texture carried by a velocity field | The feeling of a continuous current rather than separate objects bobbing |
| Cel-shaded water | Clearly separated illustrative bands of color and light | Shape clarity within the soft atmosphere |

These are search directions. Not every result will have the desired palette or energy level. In particular, a realistic fluid simulation, a combat effect, and a soft cloud illustration solve different parts of the brief.

## My proposed blend

Keep Cotton Sky's spacious light, volume, and sense of depth. Add flowing water forms with connected curves, recognizable foam edges, and tapering trails. Let the foam dissolve into cloudlike mist so those materials feel related. Use blue, azure, slate-indigo, ivory, and restrained sunlight gold, with enough contrast for clear text.

Let the large currents move smoothly. Give the drawn surface details their own restrained replacement cadence. This preserves both the smooth water movement and the earlier interest in redrawn animation.

A more useful brief than a single film reference is:

> A luminous cloud world with smooth stylized water ribbons, curling mist, and softly changing hand-painted details. Use broad continuous curves, white foam accents, layered blue shading, restrained warm light, and clear editorial typography. Keep the motion composed and fluid.

The new **05 — Elemental flow** mockup tests the shape and palette. It is a still image, so it cannot settle the actual flow cadence. The next meaningful test would be a short loop of the chosen material.
