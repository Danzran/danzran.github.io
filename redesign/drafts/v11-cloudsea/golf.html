Golf Swing Analyzer

FLUTTER · DART · FIREBASE · BLUETOOTH · MEDIAPIPE

2024–25 · Extropian

A golf app that reads swing data from wearable Bluetooth sensors and gives feedback in real time. For this page I ran my own swing through MediaPipe pose: every node, angle and the hand path you see is measured, not drawn by hand.

    How it works

    1. 1

      Sensor

      A wrist sensor streams motion over Bluetooth to the Flutter app.

    2. 2

      Pose model

      Video frames go through a pose model; joints become angles and a hand path.

    3. 3

      Feedback

      Tempo, backswing and downswing timing, stored in Firebase and compared swing to swing.
