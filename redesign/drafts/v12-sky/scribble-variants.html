Scribble variants, from calm to choppy

Every variant is the Unchained LOW preset in Peachy. Only the cadence (Posterize Time fps) and the wiggle amplitude change. A big wiggle re-randomises the edge every frame, so it jitters. A small wiggle lets the edge drift. A low fps holds each drawing for several frames, like hand-drawn line boil. Each card links to the full site with those settings.
