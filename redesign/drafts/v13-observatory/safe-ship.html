THE CONTEXT

A lot of conversations.
A need for clarity.

At Safe Ship Moving Services, calls are central to the business. Capturing them is only the beginning: the recordings need to be available, searchable, and useful to the people reviewing sales conversations.

I build the systems connecting those steps, alongside partner tooling and improvements to the core application.

01 / CALL INFRASTRUCTURE

From recording to retrieval.

I built a pipeline that captures more than 40,000 calls a day in metadata, archives eligible recordings, and makes transcriptions searchable. RingCentral is the call source; AssemblyAI supplies transcription.

The engineering focus

Capture, transcription, and retrieval are different responsibilities. Treating them as stages makes the pipeline easier to reason about than treating a call as one indivisible operation.

The daily volume refers to call metadata. Recording and transcription volume depends on eligibility; it is not a claim that every one of those calls is recorded or reviewed by AI.

02 / MAKING THE DATA USEFUL

Review that leads to coaching.

On top of the call infrastructure, I built AI compliance scoring and sales coaching. Sales calls are scored against a versioned rubric, and missed criteria become coaching opportunities.

Why the rubric matters

A model’s output needs an explicit standard to be meaningful. Keeping the rubric versioned makes the review criteria concrete and allows them to evolve deliberately.

03 / PARTNER OPERATIONS

A portal in five weeks.

I built a full-stack partner portal covering invoicing, API keys, and reconciliation. It replaced shared spreadsheet workflows with a dedicated interface and backend.

This work spans React interfaces and the server-side flows behind them. The goal was practical: give partners and operators a clearer place to do the work.

04 / MAINTAINABILITY

One large file.
Smaller, reviewable changes.

I split a 33,000-line jobs API file into 61 focused modules over 18 staged changes. The work made the API easier to navigate while keeping the review surface manageable.
